# GeoWizard

## What is GeoWizard??
GeoWizard is a site detailing a collection of flag quizzes. 
Geowizard is useful to help strengthen your geography skills!
GeoWizard was coded with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## What can I do on GeoWizard?
GeoWizard contains 3 activity selections with many activities under each one! These selections include: flag practice, quizzes, and games!

## How do I navigate the website?
To navigate the website, click one of the selections in the top right corner. On any page you are also able to click the 'GeoWizard' logo in the top left of the screen to go back to the home page. 

## QUIZZES >>
There are 3 quizzes! GeoWizard currently has a flag quiz, country outline quiz, and a capital quiz!

## TO PLAY THE OUTLINES QUIZ
To play the outlines game, To play, click on "Quizzes" in the top right corner and select 'Outlines'. Then, click whether you want to practice state or country outlines. Select state outlines to go to the state outline quiz, or you can select country outlines and then the difficulty you desire to get to the country outline quiz. To play, look at the image and type in your guess. The site will tell you whether or not your answer was correct. You can stop the quiz whenever youre done or keep playing until you hit every country/state in the quiz (the end!) When you complete the quiz, you will be taken to a 'Congratulations' page showing your total score. 
Enjoy!

## PLAY THE FLAG QUIZ
Select the "Flag Quiz" option in the top right. Then, select your difficulty. GeoWizard has 3 difficulty selections including: easy, medium, and hard. The easy level contains flags that are commonly known and of popular countries. The medium level is the largest, with harder flags. This level contains flags that many people would know, but with practice. The final level is the hard level. This is a challenging collection of flags. There are flags of islands, lesser known countries, and flags of countries that are difficult to spell or pronounce. Enter your answer, and when you click the 'enter' key, the site will tell you whether your answer was correct or incorrect. 

## How do I stop the flag quiz?
To stop the flag quiz, click the button in the bottom center underneath the submission box. This will submit your answers and calculate your score. The flag quiz currently does not have a fixed number of questions, so you can play till you feel you are proficient! 

## PLAY THE CAPTIAL QUIZ
To play, look above the text box and think of the capital of the country! Input your answer and the he site will tell you whether or not it was correct! You stop the quiz early and recieve your score or play until you run out of countries! When you complete the quiz, you will be taken to a new window showing your score.
Have fun!

## PLAY THE FLAG PRACTICE
Select the "Flag Practice" option in the top right. Then, select which continent you wish to practice. Each continent contains islands and territories that also reside within the continental boundaries. The flag practices work the same as the flag quiz!

## PLAY THE DAILY GAMES
There are four daily games ready to play! They are the "Country Name", "Flag of the Day", "Name Countries" and the 'Timed Quiz" games! 

## PLAY THE COUNTRY NAME GAME
To play, click on "Games" in the top right corner and select 'Country Name'.
To play the country name game, type in the name of a country with the same length  as the number of boxes and click enter. Each letter will light up with a color indicating the correctness of the placement.

GREEN = Right letter in the right spot
YELLOW = This letter is present in the word, but not in the right spot.
GRAY = This letter is not in the word at all. 

** You will get 5 attempts to guess the country! If you are unable to figure out any country names, click the 'HINT' button to find out the continent of the country! ** 

** If you are truly unable to figure out the answer, you can click the 'QUIT' button, which will generate a new country for you to guess! **
Also, remember that the country names can be multiple words or islands!
Once you finish a round, you can click the 'Try again' button to try again!
Good luck, and have fun! 

## PLAY THE FLAG OF THE DAY GAME
Every day, a new flag will be generated onto the site! Go to the games tab and select 'Flag of the Day' and submit your answer! You will then be taken to a page that tells you whether or not you were correct.

## PLAY THE NAME COUNTRIES GAME
Look at the letter of the alphabet being prompted. Think of a name with starting with that letter. Input your answer and the site will tell you whether or not it was correct. You can also look under the text box to see your current score. When you complete the quiz, you will be taken to a new window showing your score.
Good luck! 

## PLAY THE TIMED QUIZ 
Select the games tab and then click on 'Timed Quiz'. You will then be brought to a time selection. There are 3 options: 10 seconds, 30 seconds, or 60 seconds. Each of these times represent the amount of time you will have to answer as many flags as you can! As the time runs out, you can check to see your current score being updated live! Don't worry if you do not see it though, when the time runs out, you will be brought to a page showing your score!
Are you up for the challenge? Have fun!
