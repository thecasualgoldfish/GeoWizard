# GeoWizard
**As of October 21st, 2026, the only things working are the flag quiz and the flag practice.**

## What is GeoWizard??
GeoWizard is a site detailing a collection of flag quizzes. 
Geowizard is useful to help strengthen your geography skills!
GeoWizard was coded with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## What can I do on GeoWizard?
GeoWizard contains 3 game selections: flag practice, quiz, and a daily game! 
!! The Daily Game is currently unavailable !!

## How do I navigate the website?
To navigate the website, click one of the selections in the top right corner. On any page you are also able to click the 'GeoWizard' logo in the top left of the screen to go back to the home page. 

## PLAY THE FLAG QUIZ
Select the "Flag Quiz" option in the top right. Then, select your difficulty. GeoWizard has 3 difficulty selections including: easy, medium, and hard. The easy level contains flags that are commonly known and of popular countries. The medium level is the largest, with harder flags. This level contains flags that many people would know, but with practice. The final level is the hard level. This is a challenging collection of flags. There are flags of islands, lesser known countries, and flags of countries that are difficult to spell or pronounce. Enter your answer, and when you click the 'enter' key, the site will tell you whether your answer was correct or incorrect. 

## How do I stop the flag quiz?
To stop the flag quiz, click the button in the bottom center underneath the submission box. This will submit your answers and calculate your score. The flag quiz currently does not have a fixed number of questions, so you can play till you feel you are proficient! 

## PLAY THE FLAG PRACTICE
Select the "Flag Practice" option in the top right. Then, select which continent you wish to practice. Each continent contains islands and territories that also reside within the continental boundaries. The flag practices work the same as the flag quiz!
